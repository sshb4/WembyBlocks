        About

        Wemby Blocks follows one number: Victor Wembanyama's career regular-season blocks, measured against Hakeem Olajuwon's all-time record of 3,830. Stats refresh every five minutes from ESPN's public NBA data, and every 30 seconds while a Spurs game is live. Playoff, All-Star, and NBA Cup Championship games do not count toward the record and are left out of the streak and game log.

        Wemby Blocks is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with the NBA, the San Antonio Spurs, or Victor Wembanyama.
